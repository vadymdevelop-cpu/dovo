Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Dovo is designed with privacy and data minimization in mind. The application does not sell personal data, does not use advertising networks, does not track users across apps or websites, and does not use third-party analytics or third-party APIs to collect or process habit information.

1. Information Stored by Dovo

Information you create in Dovo is primarily stored on your device. Depending on the features you use, this information may include:

  • habit names, categories, types, and emoji;
  • habit schedules and recurrence settings;
  • completion history and progress values;
  • goals and measurement units;
  • notes that you choose to add;
  • reminder times and notification preferences;
  • habit appearance settings, including colors;
  • application preferences and configuration settings;
  • other information that you voluntarily enter into the application.

Dovo does not operate a developer-controlled server for storing your habit database. The developer does not have routine access to the content of your habits, progress history, or personal notes.

2. Local Processing and Storage

Dovo processes most application data locally on your device using Apple system technologies and application storage mechanisms.

Habit calculations, progress tracking, calendar data, statistics, reminders, local preferences, and similar features are intended to operate without transmitting your habit database to the developer.

Deleting habits or other records inside Dovo removes the corresponding local application data in accordance with the application's functionality. Deleting the application may remove local application data from the device, subject to Apple's operating system behavior and any separate cloud or backup storage that may apply.

3. iCloud and CloudKit

Dovo may support synchronization through Apple's iCloud and/or CloudKit services. If this functionality is available in your version of Dovo and you enable or use it, application data may be stored and synchronized through your personal Apple account.

Apple operates the iCloud and CloudKit infrastructure. Dovo does not receive your Apple Account password, iCloud password, payment credentials, or unrestricted access to your personal iCloud account.

The availability, synchronization, backup, storage, retention, and deletion of iCloud data may depend on your Apple account settings, available storage, network connectivity, device configuration, and Apple's own systems and policies.

If iCloud synchronization is not available in the current version of Dovo, this section applies only after the relevant feature becomes available.

4. Notifications

Dovo may use local iOS notifications to remind you about habits and scheduled activities.

Notification permission is requested through Apple's standard iOS permission system. You can allow, disable, or modify Dovo notification permissions at any time through the system settings on your device.

Information used to schedule local notifications is processed on your device and does not require uploading your habit database to a Dovo-operated server.

5. App Store Purchases and StoreKit

Dovo may offer optional paid features or subscriptions through the Apple App Store using StoreKit.

Purchases and payments are processed by Apple. Dovo does not receive your bank card number, banking credentials, Apple Account password, or complete payment information.

The application may receive technical information from StoreKit that is necessary to determine purchase status, restore purchases, and provide access to paid features, such as product identifiers, transaction status, and entitlement information.

Apple's processing of payment and purchase information is governed by Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

6. Apple App Store Analytics and Diagnostics

Dovo does not include third-party analytics SDKs or advertising analytics services.

Apple may provide the developer with aggregated App Store Connect statistics and diagnostic information relating to the application. This may include information such as downloads, installations, active devices, sessions, crashes, purchases, and other metrics made available by Apple.

Some diagnostic or usage information may be available only where users have chosen to share analytics and diagnostics with app developers through Apple system settings.

Dovo does not use such information for cross-app tracking, advertising, or the creation of advertising profiles.

7. Third-Party APIs and Services

Dovo does not currently use external third-party APIs to process, analyze, enrich, or transmit your habit information.

The application may rely on Apple-provided platform services where required for core functionality, including:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for purchases and entitlements;
  • Apple iCloud and CloudKit for optional synchronization, if available;
  • Apple notification services for local notification permissions and delivery;
  • App Store Connect for aggregated application performance and diagnostic information.

These Apple services operate under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

8. No Advertising, Sale of Data, or Cross-App Tracking

Dovo does not display third-party advertising and does not sell or rent your personal data.

Dovo does not track your activity across apps or websites owned by other companies for advertising purposes, and it does not create advertising profiles based on your habits.

9. Information You Voluntarily Send

If you contact the developer by email or another support channel, the developer receives the information that you voluntarily provide. This may include your email address, name, message content, screenshots, attachments, device information, or other technical details that you choose to send.

This information is used only for purposes reasonably related to your communication, such as responding to a request, providing support, reviewing feedback, or resolving a technical issue.

Support communications are not sold or used for advertising profiling.

10. Data Retention

Local Dovo data generally remains on your device until you delete the relevant records, application data, or the application itself.

If iCloud or CloudKit synchronization is enabled, synchronized data may remain in your Apple account until you delete it or until it is otherwise removed according to Apple storage mechanisms and policies.

Support communications are retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your request, maintain appropriate records, protect application security, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

11. Data Deletion

Habit data and progress information stored locally can be managed and deleted through Dovo's application features.

Deleting Dovo from your device generally removes locally stored application data, except where information may separately remain in iCloud, device backups, or other Apple-managed storage.

If future iCloud synchronization is enabled, cloud-stored application data may be managed through Dovo, your Apple device, or Apple-provided iCloud controls, depending on the functionality available at that time.

To request deletion of information that you voluntarily sent directly to the developer and that can reasonably be identified, use the contact details below.

12. Security

Dovo is designed to minimize unnecessary data transmission and to rely primarily on local storage and Apple platform security features.

The application may also provide optional local application protection features, such as passcode protection and biometric authentication, depending on your device and application configuration.

No method of electronic storage, software protection, cloud synchronization, or data transmission can guarantee absolute security.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your device, Apple Account, device passcode, biometric settings, backups, and other credentials under your control.

13. Children

Dovo is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising, profiling, or marketing purposes.

Where applicable law requires parental or guardian consent for the use of a service or the processing of personal data, those legal requirements must be followed.

14. Legal Bases and Privacy Rights

Where applicable privacy law requires a legal basis for processing, the relevant basis may include providing application functionality requested by you, your consent for permission-based features, legitimate interests in responding to support requests and maintaining application security, or compliance with legal obligations.

Depending on your location and applicable law, including the GDPR in the European Economic Area, you may have rights relating to personal data that is actually held or controlled by the developer. These rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

Most habit and progress data is stored locally on your device and, where applicable, in your personal Apple iCloud account. Because the developer does not have routine access to this information, you generally manage and delete it directly through Dovo, your device, or Apple's iCloud tools.

15. International Processing

Apple or other infrastructure providers used by Apple may process technical information in countries other than your country of residence. Such processing is governed by the relevant provider's terms, privacy safeguards, and applicable law.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time, including when Dovo features, Apple platform requirements, applicable laws, or data practices change.

The current version will be published on this page, and the “Last updated” date will be revised when material changes are made.

If a future change materially affects the processing of personal data, any additional notice or consent required by applicable law will be provided.
